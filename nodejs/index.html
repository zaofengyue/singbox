The Island · Chapter One

A House
Above the Water

There is a lighthouse at the northern edge of the island. It stands against the wind, facing an ocean that has no memory.

Every evening, as the sun disappears behind the horizon, a single light begins its slow rotation.

Inside the tower lives a keeper. He knows the weather by its smell, the sea by its color, and the passing years by the ships that no longer return.

From the northern lighthouse

The Light Is Still On

If you are reading this, perhaps you are somewhere far from home.

Perhaps the night is longer than you expected, and the road ahead is difficult to see.

I cannot tell you where the shore is. I cannot promise that the storm will pass soon.

But I can leave the light on.

So when the sea becomes dark, remember that somewhere, someone is still watching.

Yours beneath the lighthouse,
The Keeper
